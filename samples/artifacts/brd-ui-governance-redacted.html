Initiative: UI/UX Guidelines & Design System (design.md) · Author: [Redacted] · Version: 1.2 · Status: Approved · Date: Mar 2026

1. Executive Summary

Live AI products lack a shared UI baseline, causing inconsistent components, rework in review cycles, and conflicting design decisions across teams. This BRD defines requirements for a tokenized design system, 168-point compliance audit process, and 7 UI-only consistency prototypes used for validation and LLM-ready design context.

2. Business Objectives

  • Establish a single source of truth for UI tokens, components, and accessibility rules across Notion, GitHub, and code.
  • Increase design compliance from 85% to 92% on live products within one audit cycle.
  • Reduce UI review rework by providing repeatable audit scorecards for leadership.
  • Enable LLM-assisted design workflows with structured, machine-readable UI specifications.
