02 Jan 2026 — Jun 2026

Portfolio Report Agent (Actuals vs Forecast Automation)

Business Analysis · Reporting Automation · Portfolio Operations

Every Monday, PMs were manually rebuilding the same Actuals vs Forecast deck—pulling project dates, task counts, and status notes from different Notion databases and reformatting Gantt views by hand. The portfolio started at 7 projects; after 2 were halted for resource crunch, 5 remained active. I configured a Notion custom agent with a weekly Monday 8:00 AM trigger, read access to canonical Projects and Tasks databases, and strict rules: reference reports define format only; live metrics always come from source databases.

Case study
  • Problem: Weekly portfolio reporting took ~2.5 hours of manual copy-paste across a 7-project portfolio at co-op start; 2 projects were later halted for resource crunch, leaving 5 active.
  • My role: Defined reporting requirements, mapped data sources, wrote agent instructions, set weekly trigger, validated output against PM acceptance criteria.
  • Solution: Notion custom agent → executive summary, forecast vs actual variance, portfolio Gantt, one project deep dive, top risks, and weekly action items across 5 active projects.
  • Outcome: Cut manual reporting prep from ~2.5 hours to ~20 minutes of review per week; leadership receives one canonical report every Monday at 8 AM.
  • Automated weekly Actuals vs Forecast reporting across 5 active projects—executive summary, variance flags, Gantt views, and risk roll-ups.
  • Reduced manual reporting prep from ~2.5 hours to ~20 minutes of PM review; every metric traces to Projects DB or Open Tasks.
